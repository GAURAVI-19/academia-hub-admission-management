About us

We are the India's largest university

At MSBTE, we are committed to providing quality education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and practical experience necessary to excel in their chosen fields. Our comprehensive range of diploma and post-diploma programs cover a diverse array of disciplines, including engineering, pharmacy, architecture, and more, ensuring that students have ample opportunities to pursue their academic interests and career aspirations. With a strong emphasis on industry relevance and practical learning, MSBTE collaborates
closely with leading industries and employers to design curriculum, offer hands-on training, and facilitate internships and placements
for our students. This industry-oriented approach ensures that our graduates are well-prepared to meet the demands of the ever-
evolving global workforce.
